Departmental Committees

  • Departmental Academic Program Committee (DAPC): Formulates academic curricula, approves course syllabi, regulates continuous evaluation, and schedules end-semester examinations.
  • Departmental Research Committee (DRC): Evaluates Ph.D. admissions, oversees candidate comprehensive exams, and reviews semi-annual doctoral progress reports.
  • Computing Infrastructure & Lab Maintenance Committee: Manages GPU server clusters, network switches, software licensing, and hardware maintenance.
  • Training, Placement & Mentorship Committee: Coordinates with the Training and Placement Cell (TPC) to organize placement training, interview coaching, and coding practice.
  • Student Welfare & Grievance Redressal Committee: Promotes a healthy, inclusive, and supportive learning environment for all scholars.

Students Community & Research Scholars

The CSE Department hosts an active community of over 550 bright scholars selected through national competitive exams. Our students lead prestigious student chapters including ACM Student Chapter, IEEE Computer Society, and NJACK (Not Just Another Computer Klub).

  • Total Undergraduate Students: ~400 B.Tech Scholars.
  • Total Postgraduate Students: ~80 M.Tech Scholars.
  • Doctoral Research Scholars: ~60 Ph.D. Scholars.
  • Major Honors: Global finalists at ACM-ICPC, Smart India Hackathon winners, Google Summer of Code (GSoC) contributors.
