Schedule of Forthcoming Events (2026 - 2027)

Register and participate in our upcoming workshops, seminars, and technical fest competitions:

15
JAN

Celesta 2027: Annual Technical Fest of IIT Patna

Venue: Bihta Main Campus | Duration: January 15 - 17, 2027
Eastern India's premier annual technical fest hosted by IIT Patna. Features flagship events in Autonomous Robotics, Competitive Programming, Cyber Security CTF, AI Hackathon, and guest lectures by global technology leaders. Over 4,000 national participants expected.
20
DEC

Winter Hackathon 2026: Generative AI & Cloud Edge

Venue: Software Labs 1 & 2, Block 3 | Time: 09:00 AM (24 Hours)
Annual 24-hour hackathon organized by the Department of Computer Science & Engineering. Teams develop prototype applications utilizing open-source LLMs, edge computing testbeds, and decentralized blockchain protocols. Industry cash prizes and internship opportunities.
25
FEB

International Conference on Deep Learning & NLP (ICDL-NLP 2027)

Venue: Senate Hall, IIT Patna | Time: 09:30 AM - 05:30 PM
A prestigious 3-day symposium bringing together academic researchers and industry practitioners to discuss advancements in multimodal NLP, speech synthesis for Indian languages, clinical AI, and efficient model quantization.
18
MAR

Infinito 2027: Annual Inter-College Sports & Esports Tournament

Venue: IIT Patna Sports Complex & Gymnasium | Duration: 3 Days
The annual sports festival celebrating athletic excellence and esports competitions. Includes athletics, basketball, football, volleyball, badminton, and gaming tournament leagues. Open to collegiate contingents nationwide.

Event Registration & Participation Guidelines

Students from IIT Patna and external academic institutions can register for upcoming technical hackathons and conferences via the links below:

  • Internal Students: Log in with your institute LDAP credentials via the ERP Portal to register for departmental seminars and internal workshops.
  • External Participants: Visit the official event portals or email the respective faculty coordinator / organizing committee.
  • Certificates & Awards: Certificates of participation and merit cash awards are provided for all competitive tracks.
