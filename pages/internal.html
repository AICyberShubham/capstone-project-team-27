Career Up: Internships & Placements

The Department works closely with the Training and Placement Cell (TPC) to prepare students for top technology roles across the globe:

  • Placement Success: 98.4% consistent placement record with average CTC exceeding ₹ 24.5 LPA for CSE graduates.
  • Global Tech Giants: On-campus recruitment from Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe, Uber, Goldman Sachs, Atlassian, and Samsung R&D.
  • Summer Internships: High-stipend 2-month summer internships for pre-final year students with Pre-Placement Offers (PPOs).
  • Coding & Algorithm Training: Dedicated algorithmic training and mock technical interview sessions hosted by the NJACK Coding Club.
