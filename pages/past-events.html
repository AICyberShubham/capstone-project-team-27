Archive of Concluded Events & Activities

Highlights and photo retrospectives of significant events hosted by the CSE Department and IIT Patna:

18
OCT

37th Inter-IIT Aquatics & Sports Tournament

Venue: Bihta Sports Complex, IIT Patna | Concluded: October 2026
IIT Patna successfully hosted student athletes from across 23 IITs for the annual inter-institute games. CSE student contingents won multiple gold medals in table tennis, chess, and swimming tournaments, showcasing physical rigor alongside computational problem solving.
24
AUG

Orientation Programme for B.Tech & M.Tech Freshmen

Venue: Main Auditorium, Academic Complex | Concluded: August 2026
Formal welcome address by the Director, Dean of Academics, and Head of the CSE Department to the incoming 2026 undergraduate and postgraduate batches, introducing curriculum, lab guidelines, research culture, and faculty mentor advisors.
12
JUL

12th Annual Convocation of IIT Patna

Venue: Convocation Grounds, Bihta | Concluded: July 2026
Over 600 degrees conferred to graduating B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. scholars in the presence of distinguished Chief Guests from the Ministry of Education and leading tech pioneers. The President Gold Medal was awarded to the top CSE scholar.
05
MAY

National Symposium on Advances in AI & Cyber Security

Venue: Senate Hall | Concluded: May 2026
Two-day national academic symposium deliberating on blockchain scalability, zero-trust network architectures, and adversarial robustness in deep neural networks. 40+ scholarly peer-reviewed papers presented.
08
FEB

Anwesha 2026: Eastern India's Cultural & Youth Extravaganza

Venue: Open Air Theater, IIT Patna | Concluded: February 2026
Annual socio-cultural youth festival uniting thousands of collegiate youth across India in debates, theatrical arts, battle of the bands, pro-nites, and literary symposia across the 500-acre Bihta campus.
20
JAN

Inter-IIT Tech Meet 14.0 Delegation & Showcase

Venue: CSE Computing Labs, IIT Patna | Concluded: January 2026
IIT Patna CSE delegation presented innovative solutions in AI algorithmic trading, unmanned aerial systems, and blockchain governance, securing top accolades among 23 participating IITs.
12
JAN

Winter School on Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing

Venue: Seminar Hall 1, Block 3 | Concluded: January 2026
An intensive 5-day national workshop bringing together doctoral researchers, undergraduate engineers, and industry scientists to explore transformer architectures and generative AI models.
