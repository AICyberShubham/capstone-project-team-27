Welcome to the Department of Computer Science & Engineering

Welcome to the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Indian Institute of Technology Patna. Established in 2008 as one of the founding departments of the institute, our mission is to develop technological leaders who solve difficult real-world challenges through computational innovation.

Our department offers comprehensive B.Tech, M.Tech, and Ph.D. programs with state-of-the-art laboratory facilities in Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Cyber-Physical Systems, Cloud Computing, and Hardware Security. Our faculty members publish extensively in high-impact IEEE, ACM, and international conferences, with substantial funding from DST, MeitY, DRDO, and global industry partners.

Whether you are a prospective student, researcher, or industrial collaborator, we invite you to explore our academic programs and cutting-edge research endeavors.

Vision of CSE at IIT Patna

Block 3: Department of Computer Science and Engineering

The vision of the Computer Science and Engineering department is to:

  • Empower students with strong algorithmic and mathematical problem-solving skills coupled with industry-ready software engineering mastery.
  • Conduct high-impact research in critical technologies such as Generative AI, Large Language Models, Zero-Trust Cybersecurity, and Quantum Computing.
  • Foster an ecosystem of technology commercialization and entrepreneurship through IIT Patna's Technology Innovation Hub (TIH).
  • Cultivate professional ethics, collaborative teamwork, and societal responsibility in every engineer.
